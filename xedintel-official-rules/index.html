Eligibility Unless otherwise stated in the applicable giveaway-specific terms, each Promotion is open only to legal residents of the 50 United States and District of Columbia who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Sponsor's records regarding submission times will control in the event of a dispute, to the extent permitted by applicable law. 5. Unless otherwise expressly stated, there is a limit of one entry per natural person, regardless of the number of YouTube accounts or other accounts that person controls. Only the entrant's first valid entry will be considered. Multiple entries, automated entries, bot-generated entries, mass submissions, or entries submitted through multiple accounts controlled by the same person may be disqualified. For Promotions using YouTube comments as entries, edited comments may be disqualified. No purchase, subscription, video like, share, membership, or other YouTube engagement action beyond the stated entry itself is required to enter or win. Entries must comply with YouTube's Community Guidelines. Entries that violate those guidelines will be disqualified. YouTube Community Guidelines: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9288567 6. Sponsor's determination is final, subject to applicable law. 7. Prize The specific prize or prizes, number of winners, and Approximate Retail Value ("ARV") for each Promotion will be stated in the applicable XedIntel YouTube video description. If a stated prize becomes unavailable for reasons outside Sponsor's reasonable control, Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater approximate retail value where permitted by law. 8. Winner Notification The potential winner will be contacted by the official @XedIntel YouTube channel, including by a reply to the winning comment and/or another contact method made available through YouTube or stated in the applicable giveaway-specific terms. The potential winner may be required to verify ownership of the account used to submit the winning entry and provide reasonable proof of eligibility. If the potential winner: cannot be contacted; does not respond within five (5) calendar days after Sponsor's initial winner notification; cannot verify ownership of the account used to enter; is found to be ineligible; declines the prize; or otherwise fails to comply with these Official Rules, the prize may be offered to an alternate eligible entrant determined according to the winner-selection procedure stated for that Promotion. 9.
